## Diagnosis and implementation

The baseline router prioritizes a design/review keyword before mixed affirmative work. Executing its unchanged `route_request` yields `deck-design` for “先写正文，再设计样页” and “Write the manuscript and design a sample page”, and `deck-review` for “审阅正文，然后设计样页” and “Review the manuscript, then rewrite it”. The natural Master reader consequently refuses the correct coordination entry.

The router now detects distinct affirmative action clauses before choosing a single role. Explicit role names retain precedence; negated clauses and review/coordination words inside the subject do not claim another responsibility. Routing remains a suggestion, never authorization.

`completion_status` previously only completed `content_only`. A `design_trial` planning-only request therefore continued into content or blueprint production. The new read-only design projection recognizes the contract's design-only scope and zero-call/zero-candidate planning grants. A proposal response reports its actual scoped output as completed without adoption. `propose` still does not change the Document: an unconfirmed proposal must be retained by its returned immutable Ref, and later `continue` does not discover orphan objects. Committed v3 recipes can complete readback only when their source snapshot has the current request, exact requested page scope and current basis. Incomplete planning returns a design action, never an implicit content/blueprint dispatch. Sample production grants are not reclassified as planning based on remaining credits.
