Dispatch previously always requested the global required-unit set, and receiver
coverage also required that global set even when only speech was stale.
Dispatch now derives missing and blocked kinds from the normal current gate,
freezes them plus historical frontier/unclosed owner refs into the immutable
packet, and stamps the same units on the Task. The packet keeps complete source
materials, Narrative, Plan and manuscript. Native execution only presents those
frozen owners for replacement, retaining their separate review identities.

The receiver requires exact frozen-unit coverage and exactly one replacement per
frozen owner, rejects unrelated/duplicate owners, and retains normal current
basis, subject/dependency and authenticated independence validation. A Task alone
never certifies whole-manuscript pass; the normal aggregate gate combines valid
old and newly accepted judgments. A legacy Task without the frozen scope keeps
full-unit coverage. Already-valid dispatch creates no redundant Task.
